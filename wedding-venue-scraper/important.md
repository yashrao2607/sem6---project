## 8. Soft Power Angle

Destination weddings function as **cultural soft power instruments**. When Indian culture is exported through wedding celebrations — palace settings, rituals, visual spectacle — global perception of India shifts. A Rajasthani palace wedding clip or a Kerala backwater ceremony travels globally through social media within hours.

**Key Argument**: Weddings are not just tourism revenue; they are cultural diplomacy.

**Citation**: Banerjee, S. *Make Me Indian: Wedding Mandap Design and Cultural Identity*. Routledge India, 2021.
