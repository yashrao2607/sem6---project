| **tourism.gov.in** | Static destination pages | No venue search/filter | No price, capacity, or type filtering; no structured data exports |
| **PIB Press Releases** | Expo coverage, policy announcements | Regular updates | Search: "Wedding Tourism" on pib.gov.in |
| **State Tourism Portals** | Brochure PDFs, photo galleries | No structured data | No machine-readable venue databases |
| **FICCI/GITB Expos** | Offline B2B matchmaking events | Annual, Jaipur-based | Wedding planner + state board networking |

### Key Gap Identified
> tourism.gov.in has **zero** venue-level search functionality — no filtering by price, capacity, type, or city. Instagram campaigns drive engagement but produce **zero queryable intelligence**. State tourism portals offer brochures and galleries but **no structured, exportable data**.

## 8. Soft Power Angle

Destination weddings function as **cultural soft power instruments**. When Indian culture is exported through wedding celebrations — palace settings, rituals, visual spectacle — global perception of India shifts. A Rajasthani palace wedding clip or a Kerala backwater ceremony travels globally through social media within hours.

**Key Argument**: Weddings are not just tourism revenue; they are cultural diplomacy. The data infrastructure to measure, benchmark, and optimize this soft power asset does not currently exist — WedVenueIQ provides the structured foundation.

**Citation**: Banerjee, S. *Make Me Indian: Wedding Mandap Design and Cultural Identity*. Routledge India, 2021. (`\bibitem{b21}`)
